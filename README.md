**LX's Drinks And Food** is a Minecraft expansion focused on enriching the culinary experience. It adds a variety of realistic drinks and new food items, perfectly integrated with the Vanilla aesthetic.

---

## ✨ Features (v1.0.0-alpha)
* **Expanded Beverages:** Introduces a selection of fresh juices and fermented drinks.
* **Fresh Produce:** Adds new harvestable crops and snacks, including strawberries and grapes.
* **Essential Utensils:** Includes functional empty glass bottles and water-filled variants for crafting.
